✦ COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

SAPNE Rules.

Keep the community welcoming, fun and enjoyable for everyone.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat members with respect. Harassment, hate speech and unnecessary personal attacks aren't welcome.

02

Keep It Clean

Keep conversations appropriate for the community and avoid disturbing or excessively offensive content.

03

No Spam

Don't flood channels with repeated messages, advertisements or unwanted promotions.

04

Listen to Moderators

Moderators are here to keep things running smoothly. Follow reasonable moderation decisions.

05

Have Fun

The most important rule — enjoy yourself, meet people and make some good memories.
